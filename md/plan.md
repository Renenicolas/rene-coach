# Rene's 12-Week Plan: Lean, Athletic, Pain-Free
Starts Tuesday, Sep 29, 2026 (week 1 = Sep 29 - Oct 4; week 12 ends Dec 20). Built from your Superpower plan plus everything we learned from GOATA, Tripp, Mitchell Saron and Kaya, checked against the research. Not medical advice.

## The goal, in order
1. **Look built:** lose fat while keeping or adding muscle, and even out your body.
2. **Be athletic:** jump higher, have strong springy legs, run and cut without pain.
3. **Move pain-free:** low back, knees, ankles, hips, neck and shoulders.
4. **Feel great all day:** wake up ready to move, steady energy, locked in.

## Why you hurt, in plain words
- **Stiff ankles.** Your left ankle (old fracture, and your golf lead side) and your right ankle (old ligament injury) don't bend forward enough. So when you squat, your pelvis tucks under at the bottom, which is where your low back pain comes from. When you run or cut, your knees and hips take the load your ankles should.
- **Too much sitting.** Hips get tight, the low back and neck get stiff in the morning, and the upper back gets weak.
- **Lifting doesn't hurt you,** so it stays the base of the plan.

**Health check (low urgency):** your morning stiffness lasts about 30 minutes and never fully goes away. At your next doctor visit or Superpower blood draw, ask for **CRP** (and HLA-B27 if the doctor agrees). That rules out an inflammatory cause. Most likely it's desk stiffness, and this plan should shrink it. If you ever get night back pain that wakes you, heel pain, or stiffness over 45 minutes, book it sooner.

### Evening release (5 min, every night)
| Area | Dose | How |
|---|---|---|
| Calves, quads, glutes, upper back | 60 sec each | Foam roller or ball, slow, breathe. Spend extra time on the left calf. |
| Upper traps | 60 sec/side | Ball between your trap and a wall, lean in |
| Face | 2 min | Clean hands or a gua sha tool. Slow strokes from the center of your face out toward your ears, jaw from chin to ear, then down the sides of your neck. 60 sec of slow circles on the chewing muscle at the jaw angle. **Never press on the front of your neck.** |

What it does: calms tight muscles and moves fluid for a few hours, so you're less stiff and less puffy. It doesn't permanently "reshape fascia." That comes from the movement and strength work.

**Symmetry habits:** chew on both sides, sleep on your back or switch sides, phone at eye level, switch the shoulder you carry bags on, and always do the left leg first.

## 5. Nutrition (cut)
**Targets:**
| | Lift days (Mon-Fri) | Weekend |
|---|---|---|
| Calories | **~2,500** | **~2,300** |
| Protein | 200 g | 200 g |
| Fat | ~65 g | ~65 g |
| Carbs | ~280 g | ~230 g |

- **Goal:** lose about 0.75 lb a week, judged by the 7-day average.
  - Losing faster than 1.5 lb a week → add 100 calories.
  - Stuck for 2 weeks → drop 150 calories or add 2k steps.
  - **Never go lower than this while rebuilding your ankles.**
### The food rule: "Pick a team" (from Tripp, made simple)
**Every meal has a protein.** Then the meal picks **one team:**
- **Team Sweet** (fruit, honey, rice, oats, potatoes, bread, juice) = a **Sweet meal.**
- **Team Rich** (butter, oil, fatty meat, salmon, avocado, cheese, nuts, egg yolks) = a **Rich meal.**
- **Don't let the two teams play together in the same meal.**

| | Sweet meal | Rich meal |
|---|---|---|
| Protein | Lean: chicken breast, shrimp, cod, turkey, egg whites, 93/7 beef, Greek yogurt | Fatty: steak, salmon, lamb, whole eggs |
| Add | Rice, potatoes, oats, fruit, honey | Veggies + olive oil or butter, avocado |
| Dessert? | **Yes**, if it's sweet and not fatty (yogurt + honey bowl, fruit, sorbet) | **No dessert** |
| Example | Chicken + rice + pineapple, then a yogurt/honey bowl | Steak + asparagus + olive oil, no dessert |

- **Veggies and a little sauce are fine on either team.**
- **The biggest "don'ts"** (both teams together): donuts, pastries, ice cream after a steak, burger + fries + shake, pizza.
- **Why it helps:** it's not magic, and calories still decide fat loss. But fat + sugar together is the easiest food in the world to overeat. Keeping them apart keeps meals filling and hard to overdo. (Tripp's reason is the "Randle cycle." The real benefit is simpler: you eat less junk without trying.)
- **Pick the team by the protein:** lean protein goes Sweet, fatty protein goes Rich.

### Your day with the rule
| Meal | Team | What to eat |
|---|---|---|
| **Meal 1, 9 AM (pre-lift)** | **Sweet** | 1 whole egg + 6 egg whites (or Greek yogurt), 80 g oats with cinnamon and berries, a drizzle of honey. Low fat, fast energy for the lift. |
| **Meal 2, 12:30 (post-lift)** | **Sweet** | Chicken, shrimp or cod + 1.5 cups white rice + pineapple or other fruit. The most carbs of the day. Creatine here. |
| **Meal 3, 7 PM (dinner)** | **Your choice** | **Rich night:** NY strip or salmon + roasted veggies + olive oil, **no dessert** (a small sweet potato is OK). **Sweet night:** lean protein + potatoes or rice + veggies, then the yogurt bowl. |
| **9 PM bowl (optional)** | **Sweet only** | Greek yogurt or cottage cheese + fruit + honey. **Only on Sweet-dinner nights.** On Rich nights, have plain cottage cheese instead, or nothing. |

- **Eating order:** veggies first, then protein, then carbs.
- **About 300 "fun" calories a day:** best on a Sweet day as something sweet, or on a Rich night as a few squares of dark chocolate. Just not a sweet and fatty treat on top of a fatty meal.
- **Salt:** a pinch in your morning water and normal salt on food every day. Avoid big salty spikes (takeout, fries, cured meats) the night before you want to look sharp. Cutting salt to zero backfires.

- **Timing:**
  - Meal 1 (9 AM, pre-lift): Sweet, low fat.
  - Meal 2 (12:30, post-lift): Sweet, most carbs of the day.
  - Meal 3 (7 PM): Rich or Sweet, your choice.
  - Optional 9 PM bowl: only after a Sweet dinner.
- **How to eat:**
  - Veggies first, then protein, then carbs.
  - Stop at about 80% full.
  - Track for the first 7 days, then eyeball it.
- **"Eat what I want":** up to about 300 calories a day of anything you like, as long as protein and total calories land. That's how you stay on a cut for 12 weeks.
- **Weekend drinking (one night):**
  - Max about 3-4 drinks, and count them (about 100-150 calories each). Take them from that day's carbs and fat, not your protein.
  - Eat a protein meal first, and have a water between drinks.
  - No sauna that night.
  - Next day: electrolytes, a walk, normal meals, no heavy legs.
- **Metabolism in one line:** more muscle + more steps + enough protein + good sleep + no crash dieting. That's what actually raises it.
- **Supplements:** keep your Superpower stack as is.

## 6. Pain rules
| Pain (0-10) | Action |
|---|---|
| 0-2 | Keep going |
| 3 | OK only if it's gone by the next morning |
| Above 3, or worse the next morning | Cut that exercise 20-30% (weight, depth or jumps) and recheck in 2-3 days |

Sharp pain always means stop or swap. Change one big thing per week. Every 4th week (weeks 4, 8, 12), cut sets by a third.
