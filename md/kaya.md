### The "Morning Method" protocol (his "3 Morning Non-Negotiables," free guide `kaya_files/morning_method_figma.txt`; this is also Classroom Energy Optimizers lessons 1-3)
1. **Lymph drainage + morning light, about 10 minutes, outside and barefoot, any weather.** Hit 7 spots, doing both sides: under the collarbone/upper chest, collarbone, armpits, neck below the ear behind the jaw angle, upper belly/navel, groin crease, and behind each knee. For each spot, do **10-15 hard taps, then 10-15 circles clockwise and 10-15 counterclockwise.** On the belly, use both hands stacked, tap/slap extra hard, and circle **clockwise only**, 10-20 times. Finish with **50-100 small, loose hops** "to fully drain the system."
2. **Phone stays in airplane mode until you have: done the lymph routine, drunk water with minerals, and done 2-5+ minutes of breathwork or meditation.** Breathwork options: box breathing (4-4-4-4 for 3-5 min) or alternate-nostril breathing (5-10 min). Meditation: start at 5 min seated (not lying down), focus on the breath at the nose tip, and let thoughts pass ("Where did this thought come from? Where does it go?"). He recommends the Waking Up app's 30-day course.
3. **Minerals, light, air:** On waking, 16-20 oz water (spring or reverse-osmosis water) plus a pinch of Celtic, Redmond, or Baja Gold salt. Get 5-20 min of direct outdoor light within 30 min of waking (no sunglasses, no window glass), again at midday (10am-2pm), and watch the sunset. Sleep with a window cracked and nose-breathe.
- In the feed, members added grounding, tai chi-style movement, mobility, and cold showers (e.g. Daniel Romano's "Mornings" post, /kaya/mornings).

### Rebuilt fat-loss ("Shred") protocol, from his 33-min video (captions in `tx/kaya/cc/post_how-to-lose-fat-in-3-weeks_0.txt`)
1. Find your TDEE with an online calculator. Ignore watch calorie numbers ("most of the time not accurate at all").
2. Track what you eat for 1-2 normal days (labels, notes app) to see where you really are.
3. Eat about **500 kcal below TDEE.** Goal: lose 3-5 lb of fat in 21 days.
4. **Protein: 1 g per lb of goal body weight, and try to go over it.** Examples: 1 lb of 93/7 beef ≈ 680 kcal and about 90 g protein. 1 lb of 99% lean turkey is under 500 kcal. A full tub of Siggi's nonfat skyr ≈ 400 kcal and 76 g protein.
5. **Use the thermic effect of food:** he says protein costs 20-30% of its calories to digest, carbs 5-15%, and fat 0-3%.
6. **Volume hacking and swaps:** 93/7 instead of 80/20 beef. Chicken breast instead of thighs. Nonfat skyr or Greek yogurt and low-fat cottage cheese (Good Culture). Extra-firm tofu. Count calorie-dense healthy foods (cacao, avocado, nuts, olive oil, butter, parm, white rice, sourdough) and portion them.
7. **Plate:** 50% lean protein, a big pile of low-cal vegetables (bok choy, carrots, cooked spinach, arugula, mushrooms, kimchi, sauerkraut), about 15% carbs (fruit, squash, sweet potato, some rice).
8. **Fiber-max:** eat carrots before, during, and after meals. Kiwis with the skin, berries, apples.
9. **NEAT is the secret weapon: 10k+ steps a day ≈ 500 extra kcal.** Walk during calls and emails, pace between sets, fidget.
10. **Training:** lift heavy 3-4 times a week for 45 min, close to failure. Pair it with walking, not running (he says running raises hunger).
11. **Morning and midday light for appetite control.** Blue blockers at night.
12. **Drink water with electrolytes** ("half the time you're hungry you're just thirsty"). **No alcohol, no liquid calories, no eating on your phone.** Watch out for weekend binges ("a bad Saturday can delete 5-6 days of deficit"). When eating out, order lean cuts, sauces on the side, and double protein.
- Free slide deck: https://barn-snout-72336535.figma.site. Lean swaps PDF: `kaya_files/lean_grocery_guide.txt`.

## 3. Teaching by topic

### A. Nutrition and protein
1. **Protein first, 35 g+ in the first meal.**
   - How: 3 eggs + Greek yogurt, steak and eggs, cottage cheese + smoked salmon, a turkey scramble, 1 lb of ground turkey ("more than 100 g protein, 480 kcal").
   - Why he says it works: steadier blood sugar, fewer cravings, keeps muscle.
   - Source: /kaya/the-protein-first-challenge-starts-monday-feb-3, /kaya/more-than-100-grams-to-start-the-day
   - Verdict: **Solid.** High-protein breakfasts reliably increase fullness. Members reported fewer sugar cravings and not needing snacks.
2. **Daily protein of 1 g per lb of goal weight.**
   - For Rene: about 190-200 g/day.
   - Verdict: **Solid** for someone who lifts 5 times a week (the evidence plateau is about 0.7-1 g/lb).
3. **Thermic effect of food numbers** (protein 20-30%, carbs 5-15%, fat 0-3%).
   - Verdict: **Solid.** These match textbook ranges.
4. **Lean-swap grocery list** (from his "Fat Loss Grocery Haul," /kaya/fat-loss-grocery-haul-and-my-first-shred-bowl):
   - Pantry and fridge: bone-in wild sardines in water; nonfat unflavored skyr or Greek yogurt; low-fat cottage cheese; cinnamon; frozen berries; kiwi, melons, stone fruit; sweet potato, shiitake, squash; cooking greens; raw vegetables; kimchi, sauerkraut, pickles.
   - Freezer: wild salmon, tuna, and cod; 93/7 grass-fed beef; 93-99% lean pasture turkey; chicken breast; pasture-raised eggs ("not free range, not cage-free"); sprouted extra-firm tofu.
   - Verdict: **Solid** as a fat-loss tool. The "pasture-raised only" quality rules are **plausible or a preference.**
5. **Cinnamon "for insulin sensitivity"** (in every bowl).
   - Verdict: **Plausible but small.** Effects in trials are weak and inconsistent. Harmless, and it is free flavor.
6. **Fats: keep processed snacks low-fat and get fats from whole foods** ("fats become you"; hormones and skin are made from dietary fat). From the on-the-go bowl video.
   - Verdict: **Plausible idea, overstated mechanism.**
7. **Seasonal eating:** avoid tropical fruit in winter. Buy local from the farmers market and ask if it was sprayed. Wash produce in RO water + baking soda (Truly Fruit Wash).
   - Source: /kaya/lets-banter comments
   - Verdict: baking-soda wash **solid** (a lab study found it beats plain water for surface pesticides). Seasonal eating **unproven.**
8. **Oxalates:** always cook spinach; the oxalates leach into the water.
   - Verdict: **Solid** (boiling cuts soluble oxalate).
9. **Carnivore diet:** "beneficial as a temporary elimination diet and anti-inflammatory protocol."
   - Verdict: **Plausible short-term, unproven long-term.**
10. **Sugar cravings = deficiencies + stress + poor sleep.**
    - Deficiencies he blames: magnesium (cacao, pumpkin seeds), iron, zinc (oysters), calcium, and B vitamins.
    - Stress: he names glycine, "~10 g/day from connective tissue."
    - Sleep: less sleep means more ghrelin and less leptin.
    - Source: /kaya/heres-why-you-might-be-craving-sugar
    - Verdict: the sleep-to-appetite link is **solid**. The deficiency link is **plausible but weak**. The "10 g glycine" figure is **unproven** (it comes from one theoretical paper).
11. **Monk fruit is OK in a cut; avoid aspartame and sucralose.**
    - Verdict: **Plausible.** The sucralose worry is debated.
12. **The Yogurt Bowl formula** (PDF `kaya_files/Club Kaya Healthy Yogurt Bowls.txt`):
    - 200-250 g nonfat Greek yogurt + 0.5-1 scoop protein + fruit + one sweetener (raw honey, maple, monk fruit, allulose) + texture (sprouted oats, crushed rice cakes, chia, flax, basil seeds, cacao nibs, nut butter).
    - Timing: breakfast, 60-90 min before training, or after training.
    - Mitchell's "Ascension Bowl": nonfat grass-fed Greek yogurt, A2 whey + collagen, raw honey, bee pollen, sea moss, blue spirulina (/kaya/todays-ascension-bowl).
    - "Espresso yogurt": nonfat Greek yogurt + espresso + cinnamon, 20-30 min in the freezer, ≈ 30 g protein and 220 kcal (/kaya/make-espresso-yogurt-now).
    - Verdict: **Solid** as an easy high-protein meal. The superfood add-ons are **unproven extras.**

### B. Sleep (Night Mode, "11 steps")
Source: /kaya/the-last-sleep-protocol-youll-need-11-steps-full-video-below, video captions, free `sleep_guide.txt`.

1. **Mobility before bed:** seiza sit (toes together, heels apart, gentle arch) and **legs up the wall for 10 min.**
   - Verdict: **Plausible** (relaxing, low risk). **Ankle warning on seiza, see Fascia.**
2. **Same bedtime every night.**
   - Verdict: **Solid.**
3. **Cut blue light after sunset:** red/amber glasses (Ra Optics) from about 8pm, phone red mode, incandescent or red bulbs, candles. If you must use the phone, long-form content only.
   - Verdict: dimming light at night is **solid**. Glasses specifically are **plausible/mixed** (small trials). His claim that "clear blue blockers are a scam; must be red/amber" is **plausible.**
4. **Magnesium glycinate 200-600 mg + glycine 3 g before bed** (PreSleep powder has both).
   - Verdict: glycine 3 g is **plausible** (small trials show better sleep quality). Magnesium is **plausible**; best evidence is in people who are low. **Doses over 350 mg of supplemental magnesium can cause loose stools.**
5. **Morning light sets the "~14-hour melatonin timer."**
   - Verdict: **Solid.**
6. **Pitch-dark room**, cover every LED.
   - Verdict: **Solid** (light at night links to worse sleep and metabolic markers).
7. **Cold room, 67-69°F.**
   - Verdict: **Solid.**
8. **Stop eating 1.5-3 h before bed.**
   - Verdict: **Plausible/solid.**
9. **If you wake at night, it is probably blood sugar.** Pair carbs with protein and fiber at dinner. Warm milk or Greek yogurt + berries.
   - Verdict: **Plausible** for some people. Usually not the main cause.
10. **Strategic carbs at night** (white rice, potato, sourdough) to feel sleepy.
    - Verdict: **Plausible** (some small trials).
11. **Breathwork audios:** After-Work Wind Down, Easy Sleep, Down Regulate (Google Drive links in the post).
    - Verdict: **Plausible.**
- **Bonus tip: "a pinch of baking soda on your tongue before bed" for less night peeing.** Mitchell admits he doesn't know why it works.
  - Verdict: **Unproven.** Adds sodium. Skip it.
- **Mouth tape (ZzzTape) about 50% of nights; silk eye mask.** "6 hours with these feels like 10."
  - Verdict: mouth tape is **unproven/weak** (only small studies). Do not use it if your nose is blocked. The eye mask is **solid.**
- **Hot shower 1-2 h before bed; sauna when possible.**
  - Verdict: **Solid** (a warm bath/shower 1-2 h before bed shortens time to fall asleep).
- **Don't take melatonin by default** (from the guide).
  - Verdict: **Plausible.**
- **After a bad night or jet lag: 15-25 g creatine in electrolyte water first thing in the morning.** He cites that about 0.35 g/kg reverses some sleep-loss brain fog.
  - Source: /kaya/what-happens-if-you-get-a-bad-night-of-sleep
  - Verdict: **Plausible.** One 2024 study found a single high dose helped cognition during sleep deprivation. **High single doses often upset the stomach.** Normal daily dose is 3-5 g.
- **His own "bad week" stack:** 400 mg magnesium glycinate, 6 g glycine, baking soda, and an "NAC macro dose" (/kaya/sword-fish-bamboo).
  - Verdict: **Unproven stack. Don't copy the NAC megadose.**

### C. Light and circadian ("the Sun Diet")
Sources: free `light_health_figma.txt`, `stress_emf_guide.txt`, and many sunrise posts.
- **Get 5-20 min of outdoor light in the first 30 min after waking.** No sunglasses, no window glass, and he says take out contacts. Get light again at midday and at sunset. **Clouds still count** ("unless it's pitch black outside, you are getting the benefits").
  - Verdict: **Solid** for morning outdoor light. The "remove contacts" part is **unproven.**
- **Morning sunglasses block the wavelengths that trigger cortisol and set melatonin.** Drive with the window down, because car and home glass block infrared and UV.
  - Verdict: glass blocks UVB and some IR: **true**. That this matters for the clock is **unproven** (the clock mainly needs visible brightness).
- **Lux numbers:** indoors 100-500 lux, cloudy day 1,000-20,000, sunny day 40,000-100,000+. At night, keep light under about 0.3 lux.
  - Verdict: **Solid.**
- **Red and near-infrared light "feed your mitochondria"; LEDs "starve" them.** Use incandescent bulbs and Bon Charge red panels.
  - Verdict: red-light therapy has **some** evidence for skin and muscle recovery. The "LED deficiency" story is **unproven.**
- **Morning light fixes hunger via leptin.** Eating in sunlight gives better insulin response.
  - Verdict: **Unproven/weak.** One study (Reid 2014) linked later light timing to higher BMI, but that is an association only.
- **How to avoid sunburn:** a Classroom lesson (locked). His supplement guide suggests astaxanthin as "internal sunscreen."
  - Verdict: **Unproven.** Keep using sunscreen for long golf rounds.
- **Grounding** (barefoot, or touch a tree "gives the same effect"; grounding shoes).
  - Verdict: **Unproven.**

### D. Movement, fascia, and training
**Fix the Fascia: 8 videos, OPEN at Level 1** via https://www.skool.com/kaya/classroom/e7c770e1. These are his roommate Mucci's GOATA-style drills. Mitchell's posts also mention "peep the spiral walk! No duck walk!" (/kaya/end-of-fat-loss-challenge).

| Move | What to do (his words) | Dose |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Seiza rockers | Kneel sitting back on your heels, **toes turned in, heels out**, trying to get the "outer ankle bone to the floor." Pad under the knees if needed. Hands on the floor in front. Rock back slowly toward your heels without rounding your back. Load ankles, knees, hips, and glutes. "Not an aggressive drop." If too intense, walk your hands farther forward | 1-2 min |
| 2. Seiza with active toes | Same position, but press the **pinky toe into the floor**, keep the back arched, head up, and push back with your hands | ~1 min |
| 3. Flat-to-toes transitions | From seiza, flip between flat feet (tops down) and tucked toes (up on the toes), back and forth. Heels out, pinky toe down, no rounding | ~1 min, "ankle should be burning" |
| 4. Seiza to deep squat | Hands on the floor, go up onto the big toes, sink into a deep squat (knees wide, chest up, **toes in, about a fist between the feet**), then return to seiza. Slow and controlled | 10 reps |
| 5. Squat to inward-rotation stand | From the deep squat, turn toes and knees inward and try to lock out the legs while staying off the heels, then control back down. A box, bench, or chair helps at first | 10 reps |
| 6. Pigeon-toed "pinky to pinky" hip shift | Toes in, fold forward. Stick one hip out and reach the opposite hand to the opposite pinky toe, locking the leg you move away from. "What Michael Jordan did before games." Add a lat reach | 5 reps/side |
| 7. Deep-squat calf raises | In a deep squat with knees wide, toes slightly in, pinky toe down: rise onto the balls of the feet, then lower slowly "through the shake." Hold a bench or chair or lean on a wall if needed | 20 reps (beginners 5, then 10) |
| 8. The "bow" wall hold | One step from the wall, toes slightly in, knees bent, **high on the balls of the feet, knees pushed out**, chest reaching forward, hips high on the wall, flat back, chin up. Hold until failure; shaking is normal | 3 sets, to failure |

- **Verdict: plausible for mobility and foot strength, unproven as a "fascia fix."** GOATA claims have no controlled trials. Slow, loaded end-range foot and ankle work (toe and calf strength, controlled deep squats) is reasonable rehab-style training.
- **ANKLE RISK for Rene (important):**
  - Moves 1-3 put the ankle in **plantarflexion + inversion** (toes pointed, turned in, heels out, outer ankle bone toward the floor). **That is the exact position that stresses the lateral ankle ligaments (ATFL)**, the ones hurt in his right-ankle ligament injury and near the site of his left avulsion fracture.
  - Moves 4, 5, 7, and 8 need deep dorsiflexion, which Rene lacks.
  - Start with partial range, cushions under the shins and ankles, and hands taking most of the weight. Hold 20-30 s, not 2 min. Build up slowly. **Stop on any sharp or outer-ankle pain.** Ideally, clear it with a PT first.
- **Nighttime/morning mobility PDF** (`kaya_files/Daily Mobility & Nighttime Cool-Down Routine.txt`).
  - Morning, 12 moves: neck, shoulders, hips, windmill, squat + knee raise, **split jumps**, side crunch, inchworm, down/up dog, couch stretch, pigeon, elephant walks.
  - Night, 10 moves: child's pose, cat-cow, forward fold, supine twist, legs up the wall 2-3 min, reclined bound angle, happy baby, neck release, side stretch, savasana 3-5 min.
  - Verdict: **Solid, generic, low risk.** Split jumps are the only real ankle-impact item.
- **Workout guide** (`kaya_files/workout_guide.txt`; also Classroom Movement lessons 5-10):
  - Splits for 3-6 days a week. Core 3-5x/week, trained like any muscle with added weight.
  - Sprints 1-2x/week: 6-10 × 15-30 s all-out, 90 s rest.
  - Zone 2 cardio 2-3x/week at 120-140 bpm. He caps runs at 5-6 miles to protect muscle.
  - 7-12k steps a day. Mobility 10-15 min a day. Progressive overload in 6-10 reps, 1-2 reps short of failure.
  - Sample 4-lift week: Mon upper + core; Tue Z2 + mobility; Wed lower + core; Thu walk + mobility; Fri upper + sprints; Sat lower + Z2; Sun rest.
  - Verdict: **Solid, standard advice.**
- **Calisthenics at home** is great. "Consistency matters more than the clock"; 20-30 focused minutes is enough.
  - Verdict: **Solid.**
- Other movement: backward treadmill walking, incline walk + kettlebell (IG reels, still being transcribed). **Walking > running for fat loss** because it doesn't spike hunger.
  - Verdict: **Plausible.**

### E. Supplements
Full guide in `kaya_files/supplement_guide.txt`; also Classroom Energy Optimizers "Supplements."
- **Essentials:**
  - Creatine 5-15 g/day (**solid**; 3-5 g is enough).
  - Vitamin D 5,000 IU + K2 100-200 mcg with fat, in the morning. **Plausible, but test first.** 5,000 IU daily without a blood level is more than most people need.
  - Magnesium glycinate 200-400 mg at night (**plausible**).
  - Amino acids when training fasted (**weak**; not needed if you eat enough protein).
  - Men: zinc 10-25 mg + copper 1-3 mg taken apart (**plausible**; only worth it if intake is low).
- **Collagen 10-15 g/day with vitamin C** "for tendons and joints." **Plausible.** Some trials show collagen + vitamin C taken 30-60 min before loading helps tendon/ligament collagen. This is the most relevant item for Rene's joints.
- **Electrolytes:** 1 serving in 16-24 oz water, 3-5 times a day. Avoid Gatorade, Liquid IV, and Nuun. His favorite is Godspeed (ketone + paraxanthine electrolytes). Also LMNT, Redmond Re-Lyte, PerfectAmino. Salt: Redmond, Celtic, Maldon.
  - Verdict: useful when sweating a lot, like on golf days (**solid**). "Plain water doesn't hydrate you" is **wrong/overstated.**
- **Sleep:** PreSleep (magnesium + glycine + L-theanine), tart cherry juice 4-6 oz, ZzzTape.
  - Verdict: tart cherry is **plausible** (small trials).
- **"Superhuman protocol":** creatine + HMB 3 g, glutamine 5-10 g, beet root, lion's mane, magnesium threonate, ashwagandha, NAC 600-1200 mg, glutathione, NMN 250-750 mg, CoQ10, astaxanthin, colostrum, liposomal vitamin C, elderberry.
  - Verdict: **mostly unproven for a healthy 22-year-old.** Beetroot for endurance is **plausible**. HMB adds little for trained lifters. Ashwagandha has **some** evidence but some liver-injury reports.
- **"Detox" section:** liver cleanse, parasite cleanse, heavy-metal cleanse, activated charcoal, oil of oregano, black seed oil, digestive enzymes, probiotics before and after eating out.
  - Verdict: **unproven, some risky.** Charcoal blocks absorption of meds and nutrients. "Cleanses" have no evidence.
- **Travel kit** (`travel_guide.txt`): if you can bring only a few, bring vitamin D + K2, magnesium glycinate, electrolytes, creatine, and a probiotic. Delay meals 30-60 min. Tea before meals. Get sunlight within 30 min of waking to beat jet lag.

## 9. The 15 most important lessons for Rene
1. **Get outside light in the first 30 minutes, every day, 10-20 min, no sunglasses.** Also at midday on the course. (Solid; his #1 message.)

8. **Stop eating 1.5-3 h before bed. Optional small carb serving earlier in the evening. Hot shower 1-2 h before bed.** (Solid/plausible.)
