# Kaya: the 6 breathwork audios + the Non-Toxic Home Swaps guide

## A. Breathwork / meditation audios (shared by Mitchell in Kaya; all 6 transcribed)
Mostly third-party guided sessions he recommends. Full transcripts are in tmp/tx/kaya_audio/.

| Session | Length | What you actually do | When | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Delete Anxiety** | ~4.5 min | **Physiological sigh:** two inhales through the nose (a full one, then a short top-up), then a long, slow exhale through the nose or mouth. Repeat for a few minutes, spine straight, eyes closed. | Any stressful moment, before a round, before bed | **Solid.** A 2023 Stanford study (Balban et al., *Cell Reports Medicine*) found 5 min/day of this "cyclic sighing" improved mood and lowered breathing rate more than mindfulness meditation. The best one of the six. |
| **Down Regulate** | ~9.5 min | Lie down. Breathe **in for 2, hold for 2, out for 4**, all through the nose, into the belly. Count and imagine relaxing more with each number. | Evening | **Solid/plausible.** A longer exhale than inhale slows the heart rate. Low risk. |
| **The Wind Down Post-Work Method** | ~7 min | Same 2-2-4 nose breathing plus "your slowest exhale possible," then "send one unsolved problem to your subconscious" before sleep | Right after work | Breathing: **solid.** Subconscious part: harmless visualization. |
| **Easy Sleep** | ~6 min | **Box breathing in bed:** in, hold, out, hold, all through the nose (about 4 counts each), plus sighs at the start | Lights out | **Plausible**, low risk. |
| **Wim Hof Breath (short)** | ~11 min | 3 rounds of ~30-40 deep, fast "circular" breaths (belly, then chest, let go), then exhale and **hold on empty** (1, 1.5 and 2 min), then a recovery breath held 15 sec | Morning, lying down | **Mixed.** Some evidence it acutely boosts adrenaline and blunts inflammation. **Safety: never in water, driving or standing (people have blacked out).** Tingling is normal. |
| **Best Day Meditation** | ~16 min | Heart-focused breathing, then long wealth/abundance affirmations ("I am a magnet for unlimited wealth...") | Morning | Breathing and gratitude: fine. The "the universe aligns to your frequency" claims: **no evidence.** Optional. |

**For your plan:**
- **Before rounds or stressful moments:** physiological sigh, 1-2 min.
- **Evenings:** the 2-2-4 down-regulate breathing, 5 min, lying down, which fits your 9:30 PM screens-off.
- **Wim Hof:** only if you like it, lying down, never around water.

## B. Non-Toxic Home Swaps guide (Club Kaya, free, envoy-retina-10914633.figma.site)
Raw snapshot: research/kaya_files/nontoxic_swaps_snapshot.txt

**What it recommends:**
- **Kitchen:**
  - Cook on stainless steel, cast iron, 100% ceramic, glass, stoneware or lined copper. No Teflon, aluminum, plastic or rubber utensils, or plastic cutting boards.
  - Store food in glass or stainless steel, not plastic. Wood or metal utensils.
  - "Avoid microwaves; reheat on the stove."
- **Water:** reverse-osmosis filter (he says "Brita isn't doing the job"). Check local water at ewg.org. Spring water (Mountain Valley, Evian, Saratoga, Gerolsteiner). Add electrolytes to purified water.
- **Air:** HEPA air purifier (AirDoctor); open windows for airflow.
- **Bathroom:**
  - Hydroxyapatite (fluoride-free) toothpaste, bamboo boar-bristle toothbrush.
  - Aluminum-free deodorant, silk floss (no PFAS), tongue scraper.
  - Castile or tallow soap, essential-oil fragrance, bamboo toilet paper.
- **Sunscreen:** mineral only (zinc oxide / titanium dioxide). Avoid oxybenzone, avobenzone, octinoxate, homosalate. Hats, shade and clothing.
- **Skin:**
  - Avoid fragrance, parabens, phthalates, formaldehyde-releasers, PEGs.
  - Also calls salicylic acid, benzoyl peroxide and tretinoin bad.
  - Rule: "if you wouldn't eat it, don't put it on your skin."
  - Shower and sink filters.
  - Food for skin: fats, cacao, honey, "3 carrots/day," eggs, beef, bone broth, sardines.

**Evidence check:**
| Claim | Verdict |
|---|---|
| Avoid overheated Teflon/PFAS pans; use stainless or cast iron | **Reasonable.** PFAS are real, persistent chemicals; stainless and cast iron are cheap, safe swaps. |
| Don't heat food in plastic; store in glass | **Reasonable.** Heating plastics releases more microplastics and chemicals. |
| Mineral sunscreen over chemical filters | **Reasonable** (the FDA only calls zinc and titanium "safe and effective"). But **use sunscreen** on long rounds. |
| Aluminum deodorant causes cancer / "heavy metals in lymph nodes" | **Not supported.** Switching is harmless, but there's no proven risk. |
| Fluoride-free toothpaste | **Optional.** Hydroxyapatite has decent evidence; fluoride also has strong evidence for preventing cavities. Either is fine. |
| Microwaves are harmful | **Not supported** (microwaving in a glass dish is fine). |
| Reverse-osmosis water | **Optional.** Depends on your local water report. NYC tap water is generally high quality. |
| Salicylic acid, benzoyl peroxide and tretinoin are "bad" | **Wrong for acne.** These are the most proven acne treatments. Only a problem if your skin is irritated. |
| "Plain water doesn't hydrate" | **Wrong.** Water hydrates. Electrolytes help with heavy sweating (golf in heat, sauna). |

**Cheap, sensible swaps for you** (none needed for your pain/movement goals):
- Cook on stainless or cast iron.
- Store and reheat food in glass.
- Mineral sunscreen and a hat for golf.
- Electrolytes on hot rounds and sauna days.
- Everything else is optional.
